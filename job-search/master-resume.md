# Master resume

<purpose>
Everything the candidate has done, so tailoring can choose from more than fits on two pages. It is the source of truth for facts. A tailored resume picks the bullets that match a req and may reword them in the posting's terms, but it never adds a fact that isn't here or in `facts.md`.
</purpose>

How to use it:
- Each role has scope lines and accomplishments. Every accomplishment has a short ID such as `ACME-EVAL`, a **base** bullet, and sometimes **angles**: approved rewordings that stress a different side of the same work.
- `Tags` list the req themes the bullet fits.
- Mark each role as **flexible** (intro plus 3 or 4 bullets picked per req), **semi-fixed** (a set number of bullets, wording leans toward the role), or **fixed** (copied verbatim). See section 6 of `resume-build-rules.md`.

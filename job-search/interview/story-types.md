# Story types: the checklist

<purpose>
The story types the candidate needs ready for any behavioral interview. The `story-bank` skill uses this list to show coverage and find gaps. Interviewers phrase questions in endless ways, but they nearly all land in one of these buckets, so a strong story per bucket covers almost any question [Cracking the PM Interview via LinkedIn post; RocketBlocks].
</purpose>

<coverage_goal>
- Every bucket has a **primary** and a **backup** story, so the candidate never tells the same story twice in one loop.
- Each story answers 2–3 buckets, so 6–10 stories cover all eight [r/ExperiencedDevs; interviewing.io]. The same story can answer "showed leadership", "disagreed with your manager", and "met a tight deadline" if the candidate shifts which part they emphasize [r/ExperiencedDevs].
- Stories sit at the candidate's target level (set in `config.md`) unless a req is for a lower level. See `story-rubric.md`.
</coverage_goal>

## The eight buckets

Each bucket lists sample questions and what the interviewer is actually testing (the "prompt behind the prompt").

### 1. Leadership & influence
- Questions: sold another team on your idea · presented to people who disagreed · made an unpopular decision and implemented it · showed initiative · motivated a team.
- Tests: whether people follow them without formal authority, and how they handle resistance.

### 2. Challenge & ambiguity
- Questions: changing priorities · decided quickly or with too little data · a major change in the job · handled a risky situation · couldn't meet a deadline.
- Tests: judgment under uncertainty. Do they break down a messy problem, or wait for instructions?

### 3. Failure & mistake
- Questions: a time you failed · a mistake you made · misread a situation · were disappointed in yourself · couldn't juggle everything.
- Tests: self-awareness and recovery. Spend about 80% on the fix, what they salvaged, and what they do differently now. Show a gap in experience or a hard lesson, never poor effort.

### 4. Success & innovation
- Questions: proudest accomplishment · reached an important goal · solved a problem creatively · delivered an innovative solution · went above and beyond.
- Tests: the size of the impact and their specific role in it ("I", not "we").

### 5. Teamwork & cross-team
- Questions: worked across teams to get something done · mentored or helped a coworker · had to compromise · did something you didn't want to do.
- Tests: how they share credit, handles dependencies, and helps others succeed.

### 6. Conflict & pushback
- Questions: disagreed with a peer · disagreed with your manager · pushed back on leadership · difficult interaction with a coworker · resolved a conflict.
- Tests: whether they disagree with data and empathy and stay committed after the decision. At senior levels this gets probed hardest [r/ExperiencedDevs].

### 7. Strategy & trade-offs
- Questions: decided what not to build · balanced short-term revenue against the long term · chose between two good options · killed a product or feature.
- Tests: senior judgment, meaning which alternatives they rejected and why, and whether they can defend the call when challenged [interviewing.io].

### 8. People & org building
- Questions: built or grew a team · developed someone · hired or let someone go · changed a process or org that outlasted you · scaled a system.
- Tests: Director-level leverage, meaning people development, org change, and resource allocation [Lara Hogan; jinalzz on Medium]. Stay within what `facts.md` says about the people the candidate has managed.

## Have ready (not bucket stories)
These are prepared answers, not stories in the bank. Keep them in `story-bank.md` under **Ready answers**.
- **Tell me about yourself:** 60–90 seconds covering past, present, and why this role. Tailored per company in `prep-interview` [Indeed; MIT CAPD].
- **Why are you leaving / why now:** honest and forward-looking, never a complaint [r/ExperiencedDevs; HBR 1964: a conventional answer such as "the pay was too low" reads as a platitude].
- **Strengths and weaknesses:** a real weakness, with what they're doing about it [AAPC].
- **Values fit:** one story per company that links their behavior to one of their values. It's built in `prep-interview` from `companies/<slug>.md` [r/ExperiencedDevs].
- **Outside work:** one memorable story beyond the job, drawn from `personal-stories.md` [The Muse; RocketBlocks].
- **Questions for them:** 10 per company, built in `prep-interview` [AAPC; r/jobsearchhacks].

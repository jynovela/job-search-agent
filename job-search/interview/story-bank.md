# Story bank

<purpose>
The candidate's interview stories, kept as detail banks rather than scripts. Interviewers come at a story from their own angle and probe details, so the candidate needs the facts, decisions, and numbers at hand, not a speech. The `story-bank` skill adds and scores entries, and `prep-interview` and `mock-interview` read them. Every fact must match `master-resume.md` and `facts.md`.
</purpose>

# Capabilities interviewers test

<purpose>
The general capabilities PM and product-leader interviews test, by role type, and the round each one usually shows up in. `prep-interview` starts from this list and narrows it to one req, and `mock-interview` uses it to pick question types. The candidate targets Senior/Staff PM, Director, and Head of Product roles. This list is general background; the req and the company's values decide what matters for a given role.
</purpose>

## Product manager (any level)
| Capability | What good looks like | Usual round |
|---|---|---|
| Product sense | Starts from the user and the problem, makes clear choices, and explains why | Product design / sense |
| Execution & prioritization | Ships under constraints, cuts scope deliberately, and unblocks teams | Execution, behavioral |
| Metrics & analytics | Picks the metric that reflects value, diagnoses a drop, and designs an experiment | Execution / analytics |
| Technical fluency | Talks trade-offs with engineers (APIs, data, infrastructure, ML limits) without bluffing | Technical / system |
| Customer insight | Uses research, data, and direct contact to find unmet needs | Product sense, behavioral |
| Communication | Writes and speaks clearly and adapts to the audience, from execs to engineers | Every round, sometimes a writing exercise |
| Collaboration & influence | Aligns eng, design, sales, and legal without authority | Behavioral |
| Ownership | Owns outcomes, not tasks, and handles ambiguity | Behavioral |

## Senior / Staff PM adds
- **Strategy:** sets product direction for an area, chooses where to play, and says no.
- **Ambiguity reduction:** turns a vague goal into a plan others can execute.
- **Cross-team leadership:** leads initiatives across several teams, and handles conflict and pushback with leadership.
- **Business acumen:** understands pricing, revenue, unit economics, and go-to-market.

## Director / Head of Product adds
- **Vision & portfolio:** owns several products or a portfolio, and ties the roadmap to company strategy [jinalzz on Medium].
- **People leadership:** hires, coaches, grows PMs, and manages performance [jinalzz on Medium].
- **Org design & process:** builds how product works (planning, rituals, team structure) so it scales.
- **Resource allocation:** headcount and budget trade-offs, and portfolio bets.
- **Executive presence:** works with the C-suite and board, and defends a call calmly when challenged.
- **Leadership philosophy:** a short one-liner on feedback, hiring, team friction, and org design, backed by a real story, including when their approach fell short [Lara Hogan].

## People manager (any function)
Coaching and feedback · hiring and closing · performance management (including hard conversations) · building trust and psychological safety · delegation · managing up · handling team conflict.

## Common round types
Use these when the company's own format can't be found.
- **Recruiter screen:** tell me about yourself, why this company, why now, compensation, logistics.
- **Hiring manager:** a resume deep dive, then 2–3 behavioral questions and a role-fit check.
- **Product sense / design:** design or improve a product for a user group. Full playbook and mock rubric in `product-sense-guide.md`.
- **Execution / metrics:** define success metrics, diagnose a metric drop, run trade-offs.
- **Strategy:** enter a market, respond to a competitor, build vs. buy vs. partner.
- **Technical:** system or API design at PM depth, and data or ML trade-offs.
- **Behavioral / leadership:** questions from the buckets in `story-types.md`.
- **Exec / bar raiser:** judgment, values, and how they handle a hard follow-up.

## What interviewers signal
Use these to judge a mock interview [jobwizard]: follow-up probes mean they're evaluating seriously, and when an answer connects to the team's stated priorities, it's landing. A quiet interviewer or one stumble is weak evidence. Judge by substance.
